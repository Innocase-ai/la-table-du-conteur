# Exemples de Prompts de Cartes d'Atlas Continentales pour les Autres Mondes

Voici les gabarits de prompts prêts à l'emploi pour les autres mondes du catalogue, garantissant le respect exact du style d'atlas continental (échelle macro, vue zénithale, 8 provinces distinctes et capitale centrale).

---

## Monde 2 : Les Couronnes de Brume-Éther (Vaelgard)
```text
Dark fantasy digital painting, grand fantasy world atlas cartography, macro continental scale (inspired by Tamriel, Faerûn and Thedas official world maps).
Full continental bird's-eye high-altitude view showing the entire fractured continent of Vaelgard surrounded by the swirling grey mists of the Miasma Sea.
Visible landmarks across the vast continent:
- In the exact center: Astreval, the Great Prism metropolis built at the confluence of three massive rivers, shining with brilliant crystal light.
- Spread across the 8 regions: The Eight Colossal Primordial Lighthouses, glowing towering cathedral-beacons piercing through rolling coastal fog banks.
- Mountain passes, ancient gothic ruined keeps, sprawling pine forests, icy fjords, and dark fenlands.
Atmospheric dramatic lighting under moonlit stormy skies, deep charcoal, slate grey, misty blue, aged bronze and amber lantern glow palette.
STRICTLY NO modern UI, NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO borders, NO giant characters in foreground.
```

## Monde 4 : L'Archipel-Continent d'Éternelle-Décrue (Thalassor)
```text
Dark fantasy digital painting, grand fantasy world atlas cartography, macro continental scale (inspired by Tamriel, Faerûn and Thedas official world maps).
Full continental bird's-eye high-altitude view of Thalassor, an entire world shaped by the Great Oceanic Recession where oceans dropped 300 meters.
Visible terrain features across the vast territory:
- Massive exposed seabed valleys, coral desert canyons, sprawling giant ship graveyards with hundreds of ancient stranded galleons.
- High plateau islands rising like sheer 300-meter cliffs above the drained ocean plains.
- In the center: Port-Abîme, the vertical cliffside city clinging between the high old coastline and the dry seabed abyss.
- The Bottomless Trench (Le Gouffre Sans Fond) at the center-south, a terrifying oceanic whirlpool abyss.
Marine fantasy palette: deep ocean navy, verdigris copper, bleached coral white, seafoam green, wet slate, and rusty barnacle orange.
STRICTLY NO modern UI, NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO borders, NO giant characters in foreground.
```
